The Curious Little Cloud

📖

High up in the bright blue sky lived a little cloud named Nimbo. Every morning, Nimbo floated above the green hills and watched the world wake up. He loved to change his shape — sometimes a fluffy sheep, sometimes a flying dragon.

One sunny day, Nimbo noticed that the flowers below looked thirsty. Their petals drooped, and the grass had turned a dusty brown. "I can help!" said Nimbo, and he puffed himself up as big as he could.

Soon Nimbo turned grey and heavy with rain. With a happy rumble, he let the cool drops fall. The flowers lifted their heads, the grass turned green again, and a bright rainbow stretched across the sky. Nimbo smiled — being a little cloud was a very important job.
